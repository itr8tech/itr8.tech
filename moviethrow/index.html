Subtitles are first-class

Ready when the
movie starts.

MovieThrow treats subtitles as part of the title, not an afterthought. When a subtitle track is available, playback starts with it on by default and keeps it selectable on Apple TV.

✓Use embedded tracks and matching sidecar subtitle files automatically.
✓Find a matching track through the built-in OpenSubtitles integration.
✓Fallback generation helps when the title has no usable subtitle track.
✓Blu-ray PGS and DVD bitmap subtitles can be converted into selectable captions.
