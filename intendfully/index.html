Track what you meant to do, and what happened.

Intendfully is a calendar for your intentions. Tap a day to say whether you did as you intended, made a planned exception, or didn’t follow through, then watch the patterns build across months and years.

Download on the App Store

Available for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Apple TV.

Three ways to tell your day

  • Did as intended. You planned to follow through, and did.
  • Planned exception. You made room for something else in advance, like a birthday.
  • Didn’t follow through. The day went differently. You can begin again.

Made for the long run

  • A calendar for each intention, with monthly totals and a year-at-a-glance view
  • Home Screen widgets and Siri shortcuts
  • Sync across iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Apple TV through your own private iCloud
  • No account, no ads, no tracking
