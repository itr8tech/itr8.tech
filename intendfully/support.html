Intendfully Support

Need a hand? Email support@itr8.tech and tell us your device and iOS version. Here are quick answers to common questions.

How do I log a day?

Open an intention and tap a day to cycle through the three states. Touch and hold a day to clear it. Future days can be marked as planned exceptions. The tick button on each intention’s card logs today in one tap.

My devices aren’t syncing.

Open Settings in Intendfully and check the iCloud Sync status. Make sure each device is signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive on and has a network connection. Sync can take a minute, and changes to the sync setting apply the next time you open the app. If the status shows a problem, send us the message shown beneath it.

How do I unlock more intentions, or restore my purchase?

Tap the plus button on the main screen when you want a second intention, or open Settings › Unlock unlimited intentions. To restore a purchase on a new device, use Settings › Restore Purchase while signed in to the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I delete my data?

Delete an intention by touching and holding it, then choosing Delete intention. Deleting the app removes the data on that device. If you used iCloud sync, the data also lives in your iCloud storage, which you can manage in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.

I turned on the daily reminder but nothing arrives.

Check that notifications are allowed for Intendfully in iOS Settings, and that Focus modes aren’t silencing it.
